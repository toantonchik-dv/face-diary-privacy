Face Diary Support

Face Diary is a private photo diary for iPhone.

Contact the Developer

  • Email: toantonchik@gmail.com
  • Telegram: @dv_antonchik

Please describe what you were trying to do, what happened instead, and your iPhone model and iOS version. Do not attach private photos unless you choose to share them.

Your Photos and Data

Face Diary stores your photos and notes on your iPhone. It does not create an account or upload your photo library to the developer.

You can delete individual photos inside the app. To remove all Face Diary data, open Diary, tap the menu button, and choose Delete All Data. Deleting the app also removes its local data.

Camera or Photos Access

If you previously denied access, open iPhone Settings, find Face Diary, and allow Camera or Photos access.

Optional Tip

The optional tip supports development. It does not unlock features and can be purchased more than once. Apple processes the purchase.
