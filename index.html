Face Diary Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Face Diary stores your photos, notes, and created videos only on your iPhone. The app does not create an account or send this content to the developer or third parties.

Camera and Photos

Camera access is used only to create diary photos. Photo library access is used to import photos you select and to save videos only after you request it. Face analysis and processing happen on your device.

Storage and Deletion

Your data stays on your iPhone until you delete individual photos, choose Delete All Data, or delete the app. Deleted local data cannot be recovered.

No Tracking or Ads

Face Diary contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, does not track you, and does not sell your data. Optional tips are processed by Apple and do not unlock features.
